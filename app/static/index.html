About HydroGrid

HydroGrid is an open municipal water informatics platform developed to forecast spatial water deficits, bound uncertainty with 90% statistical confidence, and optimize treated wastewater reuse for Bengaluru.

Civic Anchors & Authenticity

Ward boundaries (225 wards), census population (8.449M), public borewells (7,433), STPs (39 plants), and transmission pipelines are authentic municipal GIS data. Daily demand series and failure onsets are mathematically simulated for stress evaluation.

All code and datasets are auditable under the Methods view (#/methods).
